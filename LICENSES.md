# Licenses and redistribution

This repository scaffold contains original project structure and example code.
Third-party engines, assets, songs, mods, and libraries retain their own licenses.

Before bundling any engine:
- inspect its exact source and asset licenses;
- verify redistribution rights for the selected build and dependencies;
- preserve required notices and source-offer obligations;
- do not bundle copyrighted songs, artwork, or mods without permission.

The name FNF and related marks belong to their respective owners. This is an independent
community project and is not an official Funkin' Crew product.
